# 14 — Open questions and risks

- Broaden passive fixed-probe policy only with an explicit rule/test; caller
  JavaScript remains privileged.
- Contrast passes are currently limited to opaque solid backgrounds. Gradients,
  images, canvas/SVG and compositing stay review work.
- AXTree is not actual NVDA/JAWS/VoiceOver restitution. A future AT harness
  must be a separate declared capability.
- Sample representativeness is a human governance decision even when its
  execution is deterministic.
- RGAA 5 is expected to require a parallel versioned catalog and migration,
  never an in-place rewrite of 4.1.2 evidence.
- Third-party provider updates can change results and licenses; keep exact
  version/hash/notice review and never copy incompatible benchmark code.
